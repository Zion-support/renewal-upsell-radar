## What it does

Renewal & Upsell Radar watches product usage, support sentiment and stakeholder changes to flag churn risk and expansion readiness 90+ days before renewal — with playbooks for each account.

## Key features

- **Renewal-risk scoring** — usage, tickets, sentiment and sponsor changes.
- **Expansion signals** — seat growth, new teams, feature adoption.
- **90-day runway alerts** — time to act, not react.
- **Playbook library** — save, expand, or restructure motions.
- **QBR auto-briefs** — one-click account briefs for reviews.

## Who it's for

Customer success and account management teams owning NRR.
